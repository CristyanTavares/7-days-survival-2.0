The "I'll Just Go to the Store" Myth

When a crisis hits, queues wrap around the block in minutes, card machines go down, and shelves are stripped bare. Trying to buy supplies in the middle of a panic is the worst possible strategy.

🚰

The Silent Thirst

Most homes have less than 3 liters of clean water stored. Within 24 hours of dry taps or failed apartment booster pumps, stress escalates and your family's health is immediately exposed.

🕯️

The Danger of Improvising

Using outdoor camping stoves indoors, eating spoiled food from a warm fridge, or drinking untreated water causes more injuries, carbon monoxide poisoning, and illness than the outage itself.

Family Responsibility

If you care for young children, older relatives, or pets, you cannot afford to rely on luck. Having a calm, written plan brings immediate peace of mind to the people who depend on you most.
